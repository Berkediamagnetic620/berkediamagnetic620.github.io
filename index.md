## 🛠️ How It Works Behind the Scenes

Curious about the magic? Here's the transparent pipeline:

1. **First Rewrite** – Your text goes through an LLM (large language model) set to a high creativity level (temperature 1.3). This breaks obvious AI patterns.
2. **Second Rewrite** – A different LLM processes the output again, adding another layer of natural variation.
3. **Translation Hop 1** – The text is translated to another language using an NMT (neural machine translation) engine.
4. **Translation Hop 2** – A different NMT engine translates it back to your original language. This round-trip eliminates remaining AI fingerprints.

The result? Text that passes AI detectors while maintaining your original meaning and intent.
